# The hardware licence and its boundary

The hardware sources in this directory are licensed under CERN-OHL-S-2.0. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Revisions up to and including commit `74ee04b` were published under CERN-OHL-P-2.0. Those revisions remain available under that licence for anyone who received them. From this commit forward, the hardware is published under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 only.

The reasoning is in the repository history. The software under `sw/` is Apache-2.0 and stays there; the hardware is the layer where a reciprocal licence carries weight, because a design that can be closed is a design that can be taken and never given back.
